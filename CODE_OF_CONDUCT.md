# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Playfy welcomes people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. We want project discussions and collaboration to remain respectful, useful, and safe for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and respectful in issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.
- Give feedback about the work, not personal characteristics.
- Explain disagreements calmly and stay open to correction.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's personal information without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or demeaning remarks about a person's identity or background.
- Sexualized comments or unwanted attention.
- Publishing private information, or encouraging others to do so.
- Repeatedly disrupting project discussions after being asked to stop.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove unacceptable content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. To report an incident, contact the maintainer privately through [GitHub](https://github.com/Bxat01). Please include relevant links or context and avoid posting sensitive details publicly.

This code of conduct applies to project spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions.
